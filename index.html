Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor of Physics at Kennesaw State University, specializing in theoretical atomic, molecular, and optical (AMO) physics.

Research Focus

My research explores ultrafast molecular dynamics using strong laser fields and attosecond spectroscopy. I develop theoretical models and computational techniques to understand and predict how molecules respond to intense, ultrashort laser pulses, with applications to:

  • Probing electron motion in molecules on attosecond timescales
  • Imaging molecular structure with laser-induced electron diffraction
  • Understanding charge migration and vibronic coherence
  • Controlling multi-electron ionization processes
