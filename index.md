## Welcome

I am an Assistant Professor of Physics at [Kennesaw State University](https://www.kennesaw.edu/), working in theoretical atomic, molecular, and optical (AMO) physics.

My research develops theory and computational models for how molecules respond to intense, ultrashort laser pulses. The goal is to probe and image electron and nuclear motion in molecules on attosecond to femtosecond timescales.

## Research Highlights

- **Strong-field ionization and excitation:** how tunneling ionization creates coherence and excited-state population in molecules
- **Laser-induced electron diffraction (LIED):** identifying molecular isomers and imaging molecular structure
- **Attosecond spectroscopy:** probing vibronic coherence and charge migration
- **Sequential dissociative ionization:** modeling molecular break-up under short, intense laser pulses
